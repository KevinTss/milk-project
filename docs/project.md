## Milk App – Project & Mission Overview

Milk App is the current working name for an initiative to help milk industry to lean toward plant-based solution instead of pure animal one. The project is rooted in *activism in the milk industry*: challenging the default dominance of dairy, making plant-based options more visible and accessible, and reducing the social, financial, and informational barriers that prevent people from choosing non-dairy alternatives.

### Problem Space

Today, the milk ecosystem is structurally biased toward dairy as the default. In most cafés, shops, and daily contexts, cow’s milk is treated as the normal, expected choice, while plant-based options are secondary, limited, or more expensive. This default shapes what is offered, how it is priced, and how it is perceived.

This creates four intertwined problems:

- **For people:** Anyone who wants to avoid dairy—for health, ethical, environmental, or taste reasons—faces friction: fewer options, inconsistent availability, surcharges for plant-based milk, and poor transparency about what’s actually offered.

- **For animals:** High dairy consumption depends on large‑scale animal exploitation (intensive farming, repeated reproduction, separation of offspring, and constrained living conditions). Because dairy is the default, this exploitation is normalized and mostly invisible in everyday choices.

- **For the environment and society:** Dairy has a significant environmental footprint compared with many plant-based milks, but pricing and promotion rarely reflect these costs. At the point of purchase, people don’t see the climate and resource impacts behind a “normal” dairy drink.

- **For collective change:** Individual plant-based choices are fragmented and hard to see. There is no simple way to identify and reward supportive businesses, or to highlight those that resist change. This weak feedback loop slows down shifts in norms and practices.

The Milk initiative operates in this problem space: a dairy‑default system that hides its animal and environmental impacts, makes plant-based choices harder or pricier, and offers little transparency or collective leverage for change.

### Mission

The mission of the Milk initiative is to **make plant-based milk the easy, visible, and fair choice everywhere**. To do this, the project focuses on three pillars:

- Shifting norms: challenging the idea that dairy is the default and plant-based is the “special” option.
- Increasing transparency: making it obvious where and how plant-based options are supported or discouraged (availability, pricing, attitudes).
- Enabling collective action: turning many small, everyday decisions into a visible signal that can influence businesses and policy.

Products, tools, and campaigns are all in service of this mission; no single app is the entire project.

### Strategy and Approach

The project starts from concrete, everyday use cases — where people actually encounter milk choices. The initial strategic approach includes:

- Identifying high-impact touchpoints (e.g. cafés, coffee shops, supermarkets) where milk decisions are frequent.
- Making information about plant-based options and pricing radically more accessible.
- Turning that information into leverage: helping users reward positive behavior and nudge businesses toward more inclusive and sustainable practices.
- Building a community and narrative that frames plant-based choices as normal, practical, and aligned with broader values (health, climate, animal welfare, fairness).

Over time, the initiative can explore different formats (apps, web tools, data reports, campaigns, partnerships) as long as they advance the mission.

### Initial Product Focus: Mobile Discovery Tool

The first concrete product in this initiative is a free mobile tool (currently called Milk App) that helps people locate and evaluate plant-based milk options in their daily lives. Its initial scope is intentionally narrow to validate the approach and generate impact quickly:

- Help users find nearby cafés and coffee places that offer plant-based milk.
- Show whether they apply a surcharge for non-dairy milk, and how much.
- Make it clear which places are “plant-based friendly” and which are less supportive.

This first product is a *tactical entry point* into the larger mission: it solves a practical problem for users while generating data, insights, and community momentum that can fuel broader activism in the milk industry.

### Long-Term Direction (Beyond the First App)

While details will evolve, the project is intentionally open to expanding beyond a single mobile app. Potential future directions include:

- Extending coverage to supermarkets and retail, with typical prices and availability of plant-based milks.
- Creating public “scorecards” for cities, chains, and neighborhoods, based on accessibility and fairness of plant-based options.
- Providing tools and recognition for businesses that adopt progressive milk policies (e.g. no surcharge, wide variety of plant-based options).
- Publishing reports, visualizations, or campaigns that highlight trends and pressure laggards to change.

In this sense, Milk App is not the end state but the first step in a broader ecosystem of tools and actions that support activism in the milk industry.

### Summary

The Milk initiative is a mission-driven project using digital products, starting with a mobile discovery tool, to push the milk industry towards a future where plant-based options are visible, fair, and normal. The initial app helps people find cafés with non-dairy milk and understand pricing, but the overall goal is much larger: to build transparency, shift norms, and enable collective action around milk choices.
